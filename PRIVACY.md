# byEar privacy policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

byEar is a Chrome extension by [jenyadoesapps](https://jenyadoesapps.com/) that shows the chords, notes and key of music playing in a browser tab.

**byEar does not collect, store or send any personal data.**

## Audio

- byEar listens to a tab only after you click its toolbar icon and press **listen**, and stops when you press **stop** or close the side panel.
- The audio is processed on your own computer, on your GPU. It is never uploaded or shared with anyone, including us.
- The recording you can play back after a session lives only in the side panel's memory. It's gone when you clear it or close the panel.

## What is stored on your device

- Your settings: language, note names, model choice, zoom, and the instruments you chose.
- The transcription model, cached in your browser so it downloads only once. You can delete it in settings → advanced.
- A Hugging Face token, only if you entered one yourself. It is sent only to huggingface.co, to download the model.

All of this stays in your browser's local storage. Removing the extension deletes it.

## Network requests

byEar connects to the internet for one thing: downloading the model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time, or when you switch models. These requests carry no personal data and no audio. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to those downloads.

byEar has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts. We don't sell or transfer any data, because we don't have any.
